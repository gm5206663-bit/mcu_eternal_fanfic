## 1. Eternal baseline (MCU canon, all 11 share this)

From MCU canon research [4](https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Eternals):
- Synthetic beings, outwardly human, metallic musculature/endoskeleton beneath, red-blood-analog bleed.
- Super strength / agility / durability far above human. Exact tons UNSPECIFIED in MCU — use FEATS, not numbers: trade blows with Deviants, survive throws through stone, fall from height with damage but not death.
- No aging, indefinite regeneration via cosmic energy. Drained of cosmic energy = graying, cracking, circles-etching, death. Bodies cremable by normal fire once dead.
- Vacuum survival (no suit needed for short ops).
- Infinite cosmic-energy FUEL, but each Eternal's ROUTING/expression differs by role.
- Memories backed up/wiped at World-Forge; botched wipe = Mahd Wy'ry.
- Uni-Mind capable: can pool energy into one Eternal (boosts target, shuts down donors' regeneration temporarily).
- Do NOT count as "living" for Snap-type effects targeting biology (canon: survived Snap as artificials).

**OC baseline tier (v1 lock):** mid-pack physical. Below Gilgamesh (strikes) and Ikaris (flight+blast+strength combo) and Thena (weapon lethality) in raw damage. Above Sersi/Sprite/Phastos/Druig in raw brawl. Equal to Kingo-tier in a straight fight, wins/loses by matchup + prep. His EDGE is control + endurance + team amplification, not burst.

## 2. GRAVITY — primary specialization

**Nature:** Projects local gravity fields via cosmic energy. Gold circles-and-lines lattice visible on hands/armor/ground when active. Silent (no repulsor noise). Feels like "the world leans."

### 2.1 Confirmed applications (2024 entry, earned over 7000 years)
- **Personal Lightening (passive):** hovers/glides, silent falls, long leaps. SLOW flight (nowhere near Ikaris; cannot chase Makkari). Civilian rescue specialty: catch falling bus, lower rubble gently.
- **Anchor Stance:** roots self/allies in radius ~5m vs knockback/throw. Can brace Gilgamesh-tier hits to SLIDE not FLY. Costs routing; Ikaris bull-rush still moves him, just shorter + controlled.
- **Crush/Lift Zone:** 0.2x–8x Earth gravity in radius ~10m (sustained) / ~25m (burst, seconds). Deviants stagger, human gunners fall prone, thrown rubble drops. Kro-tier evolved Deviants ADAPT after 1–2 exposures (dynamic opposition, v2.0 §57.8) — must vary.
- **Trajectory Bend:** curves arrows/bullets/rubble/Deviant leaps. Can DEFLECT Kingo-tier bolts at angle (not stop dead-center). Can LENS Ikaris heat vision slightly off-axis for a blink (evasion aid, NOT immunity — direct stare still burns through).
- **Terrain Read:** passive gravity ripple sense ~50m (footsteps, burrowing Deviants, Tiamut tremors). Hard-countered by Sprite illusions? No — he trusts FEEL over EYES, so Sprite must fool his FEET too (she can, with prep + terrain knowledge).
- **Team Lift:** lightens 2–4 allies for leaps/retreats. Makkari + lightening = terrifying entry combo (canon-compatible: they trained it).

### 2.2 Hard limits (never break without earned evolution)
- NO moon-moving, NO city-wide fields, NO black holes, NO time dilation via gravity at start. Scale = TACTICAL (meters), not strategic.
- Cannot fly in deep space by gravity alone (needs Domo/throw/Makkari-carry for orbital).
- Cannot crush Ikaris flat (Eternal frames + cosmic routing resist; best = slow + stagger + ruin his flight line).
- Cannot stop Sersi transmutation, Druig mind-takeover, Phastos constructs by gravity alone. Must use tactics + range + allies.
- Fields need CONCENTRATION + ROUTING. Concussed, drained, or Uni-Mind-donor-state = fields collapse to passive-lightening only.
- Overuse = tremors, gold-mote bleed, field flicker, vertigo, stillness need (resource debt, §20).

### 2.3 Growth seeds (NOT yet earned)
- Gravity lensing strong enough to hide from cameras (light bend) — seed, needs Phastos optics + Tiamut-scale observation.
- Personal Order VI Law ownership (a PERSONAL gravity principle, v2.0 §51.4) — only after surviving Tiamut Emergence pressure + comprehending Celestial gravity law. Cannot be picked from a list.

## 3. KINETIC — secondary specialization (touch-field)

**Nature:** On-contact field (hands/feet/torso/suit) absorbs kinetic motion → finite buffer → redistributes as motion/heat/shock. Gold lattice flashes on impact. Feels like "catching the punch in deep water, throwing it back as a wave."

### 3.1 Confirmed applications
- **Catch & Soften:** falls, punches, tail swipes, rubble hits. A Gilgamesh sparring blow becomes a shove. A 5-story fall becomes a crouch. Ikaris full bull-rush still breaks bones — softened from lethal to crippling (if braced + field + anchor all stacked).
- **Store (finite):** buffer ≈ 3–5 Deviant-leap impacts OR 1 Gilgamesh serious blow OR 10s of Makkari light taps. Meter is FEEL, not numbers in-story. Overfill = forced vent (painful) or joint lock.
- **Release:** shockwave palm, ground slam, charged tackle, thrown-object boost (pebble → bullet), ally launch (throws Makkari/Sprite clear of blast). Release is MOTION, not a beam — can be dodged, needs line/arc.
- **Recoil Cancel:** fire Phastos guns / catch Kingo-sparring knockback without stagger. Team function: holds the line while Kingo unloads.
- **Vibration Sense:** reads impacts through ground/suit (pairs with gravity Terrain Read). Knows Kro's gait after one fight.

### 3.2 Hard limits
- CANNOT absorb: heat vision (energy, not kinetic), cosmic bolts (energy), transmutation, mind control, magic, fall DAMAGE that is really pressure/heat (lava), sound beyond vibration (no Black Bolt tricks).
- CANNOT absorb without CONTACT (field is skin-tight + suit; no ranged drain).
- CANNOT store indefinitely: buffer leaks as heat within ~30s (sustained hold = burns + tremors). Must USE or VENT.
- CANNOT stack infinite hits into one god-blow: buffer has HARD ceiling; excess passes through as damage (prevents one-punch inflation).
- CANNOT steal another Eternal's power by touching them (no Rogue/Stitch power theft; v2.0 §26.3 exclusive ownership).

### 3.3 Combined gravity+kinetic signatures (his "named techniques" — techniques, NOT new powers)
- **Still Point Stance:** anchor + catch + soften = immovable rescue pillar in collapsing building / Deviant swarm. Team shelters behind him.
- **Slingshot Counter:** lighten self + absorb lunge + gravity-slingshot + release = his hardest single hit. Can STAGGER Ikaris (not KO), CRACK Kro's armor plates, LAUNCH a Deviant 30m.
- **Featherfall Net:** wide lift + distributed catch = saves falling civilians/Domo debris. Signature hero moment, low lethality.
- **Event Horizon Bluff (NOT a black hole):** crush zone + vibration + darkness (dust pulled in) = fear + containment for seconds. Named by Kingo as a joke. Works once per enemy (they learn).

## 5. Scaling rule (v2.0 §69.2)
Prefer feats + conditions + costs over numbers. Always state: unassisted vs field-assisted vs kinetic-charged vs team-combo vs Uni-Mind-amped vs overdrive. Never merge into one "power level."
Example: "Unassisted, he lifts a car with strain. Field-assisted, he catches a bus. Kinetic-charged + slingshot, he launches a Deviant through a wall. Uni-Mind-fed, he holds the volcano rim while Sersi works. Overdrive = gold-bleed + collapse after."
